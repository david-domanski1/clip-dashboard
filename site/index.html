How it works

From a 20-hour stream to a submitted clip in three steps.

Clip Dashboard does the searching, cutting and captioning. You make every decision that matters.

  1. Pick a section of the stream

    When a watched channel posts a new VOD or goes live, it shows up on Today. Drag over the part you care about on a timeline lit by chat activity, and Clip Dashboard ranks the moments inside it.

    • Chat explosions, loudness and YouTube's most-replayed curve
    • Clip length and lead-in set before anything renders
    • Nothing renders until you click Render on a moment

  2. Review the clip and approve it

    Each clip arrives as 9:16 with the facecam on top, gameplay below and captions burned in. Choose a hook, tick the checklist, and approve once every campaign rule passes.

    • Required name, hashtag and minimum length checked for you
    • Approve stays disabled and says why until everything passes
    • Keyboard: space, A to approve, R to reject, arrow for next

  3. Post it and submit it in time

    Post now or schedule to YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. The moment a post goes live, a 30-minute submission countdown starts. One tap copies the link and opens the campaign's submission page, from your phone if you like.

    • Missed is shown plainly when a deadline passes
    • Works at phone width while your computer renders

Rules the app holds to

Built to keep your campaign membership safe.

Campaigns remove clippers for botted views, reposts and AI slop. Clip Dashboard is built so none of that can happen by accident.

  • A person approves every clipThere is no auto-approve. Nothing posts unless you watched it and clicked Approve.
  • Official posting routes onlyPlatform APIs, an audited posting service, or you uploading by hand. No browser automation, cookies or private endpoints.
  • One post per clip per platformA clip goes to at most one account on each platform. Nothing generates views.
  • Real footage onlyCaptions transcribe what was said. No generated video. Narration is optional, off by default and disclosed to the platform.
  • Credentials stay on your machineAccount keys live in a local file with user-only permissions. There is no Clip Dashboard server.

Passes all campaign rules is the line you want to see in Review. Until you see it, Approve stays disabled and tells you what is missing.

FAQ

Questions clippers ask first.

Does it run in the cloud?

No. Clip Dashboard runs on your Mac or Windows PC and opens in your browser at 127.0.0.1. Clips, campaign data and account credentials stay on your computer. Scheduled posts sent through your own computer fire while the app is running.

Will it post automatically?

Never without your approval. Every clip waits in Review until you watch it and click Approve, and Approve stays disabled until every campaign rule passes. After that you choose to post now or schedule.

Which platforms does it post to?

YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, one account per platform per clip. Each account uses one route: direct (your own developer app on the official API), an audited posting service, or assisted (the app opens the upload page and copies the caption for you). Sources can be YouTube or Twitch.

Does it make AI content?

No generated video, ever. Captions are a transcription of what the streamer actually said. The one optional exception is the narrated hook intro: a short synthetic voice before the real clip. It is off by default, locked off for campaigns that ban AI, and posts that use it are labelled as synthetic on YouTube and TikTok.

Does it download whole streams?

No. It reads the chat replay and audio for the section you pick, then downloads only the roughly 90-second segments around the moments you choose to render.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Pro comes with a 14-day refund, and you can cancel anytime.
